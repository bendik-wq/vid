## 2. How Brann play under Horneland, and what each position needs

### 2.1 Tactical identity (evidence)

| Season | Key data | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 2022 (OBOS) | PPDA 6.49; xG 87 vs xGA 21.3; 95 goals, record 81 pts | Hudl |
| 2023 | Highest possession in the league; PPDA 7.38 (first 5 games); 2nd in league, cup winners | Hudl |
| 2024 | **PPDA 6.57, the most aggressive press in the league**; **421 counter-press recoveries, a league high**; 1.04 goals conceded per 90 | Hudl, Total Football Analysis |
| 2026 (full season to date) | Possession 59.6% (2nd); box touches 858 (2nd); tackles per game 18.0 (2nd); possession won in the final third 4.5/game (3rd); corners 152 (2nd) | FotMob |

**Structure:** a 4-3-3 with an **asymmetric build-up**. The right-back pushes high, the left-back tucks in beside the No. 6 to make a 2-2 base, and the rest-defence is set up 2-2-6. Final-third play is narrow and built on combinations. Out of possession the press is zonal but becomes **man-oriented in midfield**. In a mid-block the No. 9 drops onto the opposition No. 6 and the wingers narrow. Counter-pressing is heavy. The research found no evidence of a back three at men's level under Horneland.

### 2.2 Where the 2026 data shows problems

| Weakness | 2026 metric | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Defending | xGA 38.7 (9th); 1.5 conceded per game (10th); 3 clean sheets (12th) | A high line with no quick, front-foot CB |
| Finishing | Big chances created 45 (11th); big chances missed 26 (13th), despite 2nd for box touches | Needs a No. 9 who finishes at or above his xG |
| Goalkeeping | Dyngeland save rate 65.6% | Secondary issue; he is contracted to 2029 |
| Late-game press fade | Flagged by Hudl in earlier seasons | Midfield running power and depth |

### 2.3 Squad pressure points

- **Centre-back:** Helland (Bologna), Sery Larsen (Philadelphia) and Holten have all left. Diop (20, record NOK 30m signing) is on loan at Aalesund and described by TV2 as "not ready". Boakye (20) is out for the season with a knee injury. Knudsen and Pedersen are both 30.
- **Midfield:** Myhre and Kornvig were sold. Tronstad (31) is injured, Opsahl is injured and Eriksen is 31. Sørensen (7.45 FotMob rating) is the only reliable starter.
- **Left-back:** Soltvedt is 31 and Dragsnes 32, with Dragsnes's contract ending in 2026. Torsvik has played 136 minutes.
- **Attack:** Matondo (released August) and Þorsteinsson (loan ended) have left the left wing thin. Castro (30) is injury-prone and Finne (31) is on an expiring contract.
- **Right-back:** recently covered by Kasanwirjo (24, to 2030) and Cornic (25). Lower priority.

### 2.4 Profile specifications (what the scouts filtered for)

| Role | Must-have, from the system | Data proxies used |
|---|---|---|
| **RCB** | Defends large spaces behind a high line, wins duels on the front foot, plays out of the 2-2 base | Duel %, dribbled-past/90, recoveries, interceptions, pass %, progressive carries |
| **No. 8** | Repeat-sprint presser in a man-oriented midfield, ball progression, arrives in the box | Tackles+interceptions/90, possession won in final third, key passes, pass % |
| **LB (inverting)** | Press-resistant in central areas, reads transitions, two-footed ideally, still able to overlap | Pass %, interceptions, duel %, xA |
| **No. 9** | Finishes chances (xG overperformance or parity), attacks crosses, drops onto the opposition 6 when pressing | npxG/90, goals minus xG, aerials won, high regains |
| **LW** | Attacks the box, presses, narrows in a mid-block | Shots/90, box touches, dribbles, tackles/recoveries, possession won in final third |

## 3. Scouting reports, by priority

> Notes on the data. Sofascore and FotMob figures are league matches only. "pN" is FotMob's per-90 percentile against the same position in the same league. "TM" is Transfermarkt. A percentile in Iceland is not the same as one in the Eredivisie, so league strength is weighed in each verdict.

### Priority 1: Right-sided centre-back

#### 1A. Mohamed Soumah, IK Sirius (Allsvenskan): **recommended**
- **Profile:** born 15-03-2003 (23), Guinea. Right-footed, 1.88 m. TM value €0.7m; contract to 30-06-2030. Joined from KAA Gent in Feb 2026 for about €1.0m.
- **2026 Allsvenskan:** 19 apps, 1,612 min, 4 goals. FotMob rating 7.23.
  - Duels won 64.5% (p74); recoveries 5.08/90 (**p91**).
  - Tackles 1.73/90 (p68); interceptions 1.45/90 (p74).
  - Dribbled past only 0.17/90 (p70); dribbles 0.56/90 (**p89**); passing 92.6%.
  - Aerials 59.6% (p44).
- **2025/26 Belgian 2nd tier (Gent U23):** 1,800 min, rating 7.26, aerials 2.95/90 (p85).
- **Horneland fit:** his recovery volume and the rarity of him being beaten one-on-one are the best indicators available for a defender who has to cover space behind a high line. His carrying and 92.6% passing suit stepping out of the 2-2 base. He also scored 4 goals from set pieces.
- **Risks:** the 2030 contract means the fee will be above his TM value (estimate €1.5–2.5m). His aerial win rate is only average. Pace is not measured, so it needs video or tracking checks.

#### 1B. Nikita Stoinov, Dinamo București (Romania): **left-sided alternative or partner**
- **Profile:** born 24-08-2005 (21), Israel. Left-footed, 1.92 m. TM value €1.8m; contract to 2029.
- **2025/26:** 37 starts, 3,315 min.
  - Tackles 45, interceptions 41, recoveries 165.
  - Ground duels won 70.5%; aerials 59.1%.
  - Passing 89%, 86 accurate long balls.
  - **Dribbled past 6 times all season**; 0 errors.
- **2026/27:** 900 min, passing 92.8%.
- **Fit:** a rare left-footed, 192 cm centre-back who is clean on the ball and almost never beaten one-on-one. Of all the CBs screened he has the highest resale ceiling. He would allow Knudsen to be rotated out.
- **Risks:** Dinamo hold the bargaining power with a contract to 2029. His duel numbers have dipped early in 2026/27.

#### 1C. Lukas Mai, FC Lugano (Switzerland): **ready-now option, contract ends June 2027**
- **Profile:** 26, Germany, 1.88 m. TM value €3.0m. Contract to 06/2027, so he can be approached for a pre-contract from January 2027.
- **2025/26:** rating 7.16; tackles+interceptions 3.61/90; aerials 67.8%; **passing 91.7%**.
- **2026/27:** rating 7.41, 1 goal, 3 assists.
- **Fit:** the most complete defender in the whole screen and an immediate starter.
- **Risks:** Swiss wages, and at 26 he offers little resale value.

**Value and depth options:**
- **Frederik Winther (Hammarby):** 25, left-footed, 1.90 m, €1.0m, contract Dec 2027. Rating 7.43, passing 90.7%, chances created p95, 13 headed shots. He gets dribbled past fairly often (p25).
- **Rowan Besselink (De Graafschap):** 22, 1.90 m, €0.4m. Rating 7.21, passing 89%, duels 60.6%. Second-tier level, with 3 errors leading to a shot.
- **Tobias Anker (Sirius):** 25, 1.94 m, €0.6m. Aerials 80.6% (p91), dribbled past 0.10/90 (p83). Limited on the ball (long balls p17).
- **Denis Halinský (Slavia, on loan at Pardubice):** 23, €2.0m. 118 aerials won (65.9%). Passing only 73%, so a weaker fit for Horneland's build-up.
- **Panagiotis Kikianis (Adelaide United):** 21, holds a Greek EU passport, €1.3m. Ground duels 60.8%, passing 86.8%. Aerials only 45%.

### Priority 2: Box-to-box No. 8

## 5. Methodology and limitations

**How the candidates were found**
- **Nordics:** 676 profiles were pulled from FotMob (900+ minutes, rating at least 6.85) across the Allsvenskan, Eliteserien, Superliga, Danish 1st Division, Veikkausliiga and Besta deild. OBOS-ligaen and Superettan were screened on minutes only, because FotMob has no ratings for them.
- **Europe:** Sofascore season data was used.
  - Western/Central Europe: 15 leagues, players with 15+ apps.
  - Eastern/Southern Europe: 14 leagues, players aged 25 or under with 1,200+ minutes.
  - Candidates were ranked by rating and then filtered on age (26 or under), Transfermarkt value (about €3m or less) and current club.
- **Rest of world:** Transfermarkt, Sofascore and American Soccer Analysis (for MLS) were used. Candidates were filtered for EU passports and work-permit plausibility.
- **Brann context:** Hudl, Total Football Analysis, FotMob team stats, Wikipedia, TV2, Dagbladet, Toppfotballbarometeret 2026.

**Limitations**
- **Not literally every player:** this is a threshold screen of senior leagues that make sense for Brann's budget, not every division in the world.
- **Missing data:**
  - FBref was blocked, so there are no progressive-pass or progressive-carry figures.
  - Sprint and top-speed data was not available anywhere, so pace is inferred from proxies. Each centre-back must be checked on video or with tracking data.
  - Cyprus and Bosnia were not covered.
  - The NPFL, the Danish 1st Division and the Besta deild have no xG.
- **League strength:** ratings and percentiles are relative to each player's own league. An 8.29 in Iceland is not equal to a 7.4 in Sweden.
- **Fees:** "estimated fee" ranges are judgement calls based on Transfermarkt value, contract length and Brann's own transfer history. They are not quotes.
- **Unconfirmed Brann details:** Horneland's exact 2026 formation since his return, the Pedersen and Soltvedt contract extensions, and Tronstad's injury timeline.
- **Before any bid:** video analysis, medicals, character references and an agent or wage check.

## 5b. Style-fit model method

- **Pool:** Sofascore league statistics pulled on 11 Oct 2026. That gives 18,692 player-seasons with 900+ minutes across 69 leagues; 16,234 players remain after removing goalkeepers and attacking midfielders (16,349 player-seasons). Calendar-year leagues use 2026; Aug–May leagues use 2025/26.
- **Role:** taken from the primary Sofascore position.
  - DC → CB
  - DL/DR → FB
  - DM/MC → CM
  - LW/RW/ML/MR → W
  - ST → ST
- **Weights** (percentiles within role, across all leagues):
  - **CB:** dribbled past (reversed) 15, tackles + interceptions 15, ground duel % 15, pass % 15, recoveries 10, aerial % 10, errors leading to a shot (reversed) 10, aerials won 5, long balls 5.
  - **FB:** pass % 20, interceptions 15, duel % 15, tackles 10, xA 10, key passes 10, dribbled past (reversed) 10, crosses 5, recoveries 5.
  - **CM:** tackles + interceptions 20, possession won in final third 15, recoveries 10, key passes 10, xA 10, pass % 10, final-third passes 10, duel % 10, xG 5.
  - **W:** xG 15, possession won in final third 15, shots 10, dribbles 10, key passes 10, xA 10, big chances created 10, tackles 10, non-penalty goals 10.
  - **ST:** xG 25, non-penalty goals 15, aerials won 15, possession won in final third 15, goals minus xG 10, xA 10, tackles 5, key passes 5.
- **Missing data:** where a league does not publish a metric, its weight is dropped and the score is shrunk toward 50 in proportion to the missing weight.
- **League factors:** judgement-based, with the Eliteserien at 1.00. The range runs from 0.72 (National League, Uruguay Segunda, Ecuador Serie B) to 1.60 (Premier League). The full table is on the site's overview page.
- **Value gap (Moneyball):** for each role, a linear fit of Brann score on log10(market value) for players aged 27 or under. The gap is the actual score minus the fitted score.
- **Market values** in the model are Sofascore estimates. They are generally lower than Transfermarkt's.
