Regionalliga and Série D scorers

Germany's five Regionalliga groups and Brazil's Série D publish goals but not minutes or defensive data, so they can't be style-scored. These are the top 40 scorers in each, as leads for video scouting. Several have already moved up. A 22-goal Regionalliga striker who is 21 is a very different lead from one who is 31.
