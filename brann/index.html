The game model

What Horneland's Brann asks for

A 4-3-3 with the most aggressive press in the league (PPDA 6.57 in 2024; 421 counter-press recoveries, a league high). In possession the right-back goes high and the left-back steps inside beside the 6, which builds a 2-2 base behind five attackers. In 2026 Brann were 2nd for box touches but 13th for big chances missed and 9th for xG against. Each role's weights below come from those demands.

How the scores work

Method and limits

Scoring

  • Pool: player-seasons with 900+ league minutes from Sofascore, pulled 11 Oct 2026. Calendar-year leagues use 2026; Aug–May leagues use 2025/26. Goalkeepers and attacking midfielders are excluded.
  • Style fit (0–100): each metric becomes a percentile against every player in the same role across all leagues. The fit is the weighted average using the role weights above.
  • Coverage: lower leagues often publish fewer stats. Missing weight is dropped and the score is pulled toward 50 in proportion, so thin data can't produce an extreme score.
  • Brann score = style fit × league factor. The Eliteserien is 1.00; the factors run from 0.72 (National League) to 1.60 (Premier League).
  • Value gap = Brann score minus the score a player at that price usually has. The Moneyball page explains it.

Read it with care

  • This is event data, with no tracking. Pace and repeat sprints are not measured. Watch video before shortlisting anyone.
  • Stats reflect each player's team and league. A tier-3 percentile is not a top-tier percentile; the league factor only partly corrects for that.
  • Regionalliga and Brazil's Série D publish goals only, so they are on the Lower tiers page as a scorer watchlist, not in the model.
  • Values and contract dates are Sofascore's. Check them against Transfermarkt and agents.
