Free agent market

No fee required

Players Brann can sign without paying a transfer fee: those whose contracts end in December 2026, those whose contracts end by June 2027, and those already without a club. Brann's main window opens in January, which is exactly when calendar-year contracts in Scandinavia, the Americas and Asia run out.

How it works

Three windows

Without a club

Listed by Sofascore as having no team. Free to sign now and can be registered when the window opens. Check why they are unattached: injury, a release or a contract dispute.

Free in January 2027

Contracts ending 31 December 2026, common in Norway, Sweden, MLS, South America, Japan and Korea. They can sign a pre-contract now and join for nothing when Brann's window opens.

Free in July 2027

Contracts ending by 30 June 2027. Under FIFA's rules they can agree a pre-contract with Brann from 1 January, or be bought cheaply in January because their club risks losing them for nothing.
