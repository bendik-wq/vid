Why this training exists

There are only two ways people try to buy a business. Both of them are wrong.

The first is to raise the money. You spend twelve to eighteen months pitching investors who want control, equity and a board seat before you've even found a deal.

The second is to save the deposit yourself. Years of grinding, and at the end of it the bank still says no — because the problem was never your deposit, it was your structure. Meanwhile private equity buys the exact business you wanted, at a multiple you could have paid.

There is a third way, and it is the one nobody teaches: vendor financing, institutional credibility and off-market sourcing — so the seller funds the deal, the bank funds the rest, and your own capital never enters the transaction.

That's what this ninety minutes is. Not theory. The actual structure, the actual scripts, the actual numbers a lender needs to see before you make an offer.
