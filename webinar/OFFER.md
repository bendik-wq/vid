# The offer

**Registration ask:** name, email, mobile. Free, 90 minutes, live.

**Promise:** how operators acquire profitable businesses with none of their own
money — for people with no capital, no acquisition experience, and a job they
still need.

## Why it's hard to say no

Four things carry the page, in this order:

1. **The third way.** The two obvious paths (raise capital, save a deposit) are
   killed on the page before the mechanism is named, so vendor finance + board
   credibility + off-market sourcing lands as the only remaining option rather
   than as one more course.
2. **The live-only stack.** $6,940 of tooling — deal flow kit, bankability
   scorecard, vendor finance term sheet, board credibility packet, a live deal
   structured on screen, lifetime Foundations access — released on the call, not
   emailed. Attendance is the price, and attendance is what a webinar actually
   needs to buy.
3. **Personal relevance.** "Submit a real business in the confirmation and we'll
   structure it live" converts the training from content into a working session
   on the registrant's own deal.
4. **The we-pay-for-your-time guarantee.** Stay to the end, and if you leave
   without a deal structure you can use this month, we send $100. The only cost
   of attending is ninety minutes, and the guarantee insures those — which
   leaves no rational reason to decline.

## Operational cost of the guarantee

It is a real liability: budget for it and cap it (live attendees only, stayed to
the close, one claim per person, claimed within 48 hours by reply). Historically
the claim rate on this kind of offer is low, but price the campaign as though it
won't be.

## Downstream

The webinar closes into Owners Academy enrolment; Foundations given free on the
call is the on-ramp, not the product. OTO sequencing (Capability Accelerator →
Capital Module → G&L done-with-you retainer) is unchanged by this page.
