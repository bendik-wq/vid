# 90-day content plan

Sequenced by the belief audit: the two weakest beliefs are proof problems, so
the first thirty days build proof assets rather than distribution volume.
Running ads before that is paying to expose a credibility gap.

## Phase 1 — Days 1–30: build the proof, put the face on camera

**Goal:** make *"the problem is solvable"* and *"your offer solves it"*
defensible in ten minutes of a stranger's research.

| Workstream | Output | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer research | 20 recorded conversations — 10 buyers, 10 prospects who didn't buy. Verbatim language logged. | Founder |
| Flagship teardown | One long-form video walking a completed no-money-down structure end to end: purchase price, vendor note, senior debt, DSCR, what the seller got. Redacted, not vague. | Founder + advisor |
| ICP case studies | Four member stories, one per avatar, each opening at the member's starting point | Content lead |
| Proof inventory | Every claim we make, with its substantiation filed. See `proof-engine.md`. | Ops |
| Channel setup | Founder channel live: YouTube long-form + shorts, LinkedIn, IG, X. One face, consistent. | Content lead |
| Offer reposition | Every asset rewritten to lead with the transformation, mechanism demoted to reason-to-believe | Founder |

**Cadence:** 3 shorts/day across platforms, 1 long-form/week, 3 emails/week.
Ad spend this phase: research budget only.

## Phase 2 — Days 31–60: volume and the grid

**Goal:** cover the creative matrix, find the first winners, fill the webinar.

| Workstream | Output |
|---|---|
| Creative production | 15 labelled creatives/week against the 72-cell grid |
| Long-form | 2 videos/week: one mechanism, one teardown |
| Webinar | Weekly live training, every week, no exceptions. The cadence is the asset. |
| Email | 3/week to the full list + the registration and no-show sequences |
| Paid | Open at a deliberately small daily budget on the four best organic performers. Organic winners first, always — never test cold on unproven creative. |
| Peer review | Weekly creative review; kill and scale decisions on data, not taste |

## Phase 3 — Days 61–90: scale what survived, build the long game

| Workstream | Output |
|---|---|
| Paid | Scale winners; three fresh variants per winner weekly |
| Nurture | The 12-month re-offer calendar goes live (below) |
| Proof compounding | Every cohort milestone captured the day it happens |
| Second face | A second presenter on camera — advisor or member. Reduces key-person risk and tests whose face converts |
| Offer iteration | First full review of the ladder against real close-rate data |

## The 12-month re-offer calendar — nobody gets burned

A lead who doesn't buy is not a dead lead; they're a lead whose timing is wrong.
Acquisition decisions run on life events — bonus, redundancy, a birthday with a
zero in it. Stay present until one lands.

| Month | Touch | Ask |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Webinar + 5-email close sequence | Enrol |
| 1 | Teardown of a live deal | Soft — reply with your sector |
| 2 | "Where you'd be if you'd started" retrospective | Book a call |
| 3 | New cohort opening | Enrol |
| 4–5 | Pure value: lender criteria update, market pricing | None |
| 6 | Six-month member results | Enrol |
| 7–8 | Segment by what they clicked; angle-matched sequence | Soft |
| 9 | Cohort opening + price increase notice | Enrol |
| 10–11 | Value + community invitation | None |
| 12 | Anniversary email: "a year ago you registered" | Enrol |

Three enrol asks a year against eight value touches. The ratio is what makes
the list still open your email in month twelve.

## Metrics

Starting targets — replace each with your actuals as soon as you have 30 days
of data. Anyone quoting these as facts before then is guessing.

| Metric | Target | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Hook rate (3s view) | >25% | Diagnoses the first frame, nothing else |
| Cost per registration | Set from your CAC ceiling | |
| Registration → live attendance | 35–45% | The $100 guarantee and the pre-delivered scorecard both exist to move this |
| Attendance → application | 8–15% | Below this, the close or the offer is wrong |
| Application → enrolment | 25–40% | Below this, the wrong people are applying — a targeting problem, not a sales problem |
| Refund rate | <10% | Above it, the sale is outrunning the product |
| Milestone completion at day 30 | >70% | The leading indicator for the guarantee liability. Watch this one hardest. |

**The number that governs the ladder:** milestone completion. The conditional
guarantee is only affordable if members actually do the work, and content that
sells past someone's readiness shows up here first — a month before it shows up
in refunds.
