# Creative matrix and hook bank

Test exhaustively, not randomly: avatar × sub-avatar × angle. Every creative
shipped is one cell of this grid, labelled, so a loss tells you which
dimension failed rather than leaving you guessing.

## Avatars and sub-avatars

| Avatar | Sub-avatars |
|---|---|
| **Corporate Prisoner** — senior employee, no ownership | (a) 15+ years, golden handcuffs · (b) recently made redundant · (c) passed over for promotion |
| **Trapped Owner** — one business, works in it | (a) plateaued revenue · (b) wants to exit but unsellable · (c) wants a second business |
| **One-Business Ceiling** — capable operator, capital-poor | (a) strong operator, no balance sheet · (b) tried and got bank-rejected · (c) has a deal on the table now |
| **HNW Capital Deployer** — has capital, no deal flow | (a) property investor diversifying · (b) post-exit founder · (c) family office adjacent |

## Angles

1. **Mechanism** — how a no-money-down structure actually works
2. **Enemy** — brokers, auctions, PE, the "save a deposit" advice
3. **Proof** — a real deal, a real structure, a real member
4. **Contrarian** — "your deposit was never the problem"
5. **Fear/cost of inaction** — the year you spend not owning
6. **Identity** — owner vs employee, what you become

4 avatars × 3 sub-avatars × 6 angles = **72 cells.** At 15 creatives a week
you cover the grid in five weeks, then iterate on winners.

## Testing protocol

- **Volume:** 15 new creatives shipped weekly. Non-negotiable — the winner is
  rarely in the first nine.
- **Labelling:** `avatar-subavatar-angle-hookID-v#`. No unlabelled creative runs.
- **Kill criteria:** below 1% hook rate at 2,000 impressions, or CPL above 2×
  target at 50% of the target CPL spend. Kill without sentiment.
- **Scale criteria:** any creative at target CPL for three consecutive days gets
  duplicated into three variants — new hook, new first frame, same body.
- **Review:** one weekly session, the author never signs off their own work.
- **Compliance gate:** every claim is substantiated on file before the creative
  goes live. This is the one gate that speed doesn't get to skip.

## Hook bank

**Mechanism**
1. "The seller funded 60% of my acquisition. Here's the clause that did it."
2. "This is a real term sheet from a deal that closed with zero buyer capital. Let me read it to you."
3. "There are three ways to fund a business purchase. You've only heard of one."
4. "A bank will lend against the business's cash flow, not your savings. Most buyers never learn this."

**Enemy**
5. "A broker's job is to get the seller the highest price. Whose side do you think they're on?"
6. "If you're bidding at auction, you've already lost — you're competing with private equity's money."
7. "'Save up a deposit first' is the most expensive advice in business."
8. "Fifty identical offers land on a seller's desk. Yours is one of them. Here's how to not be."

**Proof**
9. "This business does £480k EBITDA. Here's exactly how it was bought with none of the buyer's money."
10. "He was a regional manager nine months ago. Today he owns the company's supplier."
11. "Three offers, two rejections, one accepted LOI. Here's what changed on the third."

**Contrarian**
12. "Your deposit was never the problem. Your structure was."
13. "Banks don't reject buyers. They reject structures."
14. "You don't need to find a good business. You need to find a tired owner."
15. "The best acquisitions I've seen were businesses nobody else wanted to run."

**Cost of inaction**
16. "A year of saving gets you a deposit. A year of approaching owners gets you a business."
17. "Every month you wait, a retiring owner sells to someone who asked first."
18. "The cost of not buying isn't zero. It's the cash flow you didn't own."

**Identity**
19. "There are two kinds of people at your company: the ones who work there and the one who owns it."
20. "Your salary is someone else's cost line. Think about that."
21. "I stopped trying to get promoted and started trying to get bought in. Everything changed."

Each hook gets a first-frame treatment and a named face. Hooks are the highest
-leverage 15 words in the business — write 20 more before you write anything else.
