# The six beliefs

All six must be installed before anyone buys. Missing one kills the sale
regardless of how good the other five are. Every content asset is tagged with
the belief it installs; a channel that only ever installs one belief is a
channel that doesn't convert.

| Belief | What it sounds like when missing | Asset that installs it | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| **They like you** | "Bit slick for me" | Face-to-camera, unpolished, opinionated. Founder's own acquisition story including what went wrong. | Founder |
| **They trust you** | "Everyone says this" | Appearing with lenders, advisors, members. Named bench. Public teardowns where we're wrong out loud. | Founder + advisors |
| **The problem is solvable** | "Nobody buys a business with no money" | Mechanism explainers: a real structure, a real DSCR, a real vendor note. Shown, not claimed. | Content lead |
| **Your offer solves it** | "Sounds great for you, not for me" | Member journeys segmented by starting point — the corporate exec, the one-business owner, the capital holder. | Content lead |
| **Better than alternatives** | "I'll just use a broker / do an MBA / read the book" | Explicit comparisons: broker vs off-market, SBA course vs lender desk, DIY vs counsel-reviewed paper. | Founder |
| **Act now** | "Maybe next year" | Cohort dates, capacity, price-rises-with-proof, and the cost of a year not owning. | Marketing |

## Audit: where the ladder currently stands

Run this quarterly. Score each belief 1–5 on the strength of evidence a cold
prospect can find in ten minutes of looking.

| Belief | Current state | Biggest gap |
|---|---|---|
| Like | Thin — no consistent face-led content | No regular founder channel |
| Trust | Medium — advisory arm is real credibility, largely unshown | Lenders and advisors never appear on camera |
| Solvable | Weak from cold — the claim sounds impossible | No public teardown of a completed no-money-down structure |
| Offer solves it | Weak — no segmented member proof | No case study per ICP |
| Better than alternatives | Unaddressed | Nothing comparing us to brokers or to doing nothing |
| Act now | Structural, not yet published | Cohort dates and caps not public |

**Read:** the two weakest beliefs — *solvable* and *offer solves it* — are both
proof problems, not copy problems. That's what the first 30 days of the content
plan are for.
