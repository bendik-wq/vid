# The offer ladder

Four rungs. Each one solves the next obstacle and qualifies the buyer for the
one above it. Naming follows the MAGIC formula (Magnetic reason why · Avatar ·
Goal · Interval · Container).

---

## Rung 1 — Free: *The No-Money-Down Acquisition Blueprint*

**Full name:** Free 90-Minute Live Training: The No-Money-Down Acquisition
Blueprint for Employed Professionals
(reason why: free · avatar: employed professionals · goal: acquisition without
capital · interval: 90 minutes · container: live training)

**Job:** convert cold traffic to a registered, attending, phone-verified lead.

**The ask:** name, email, mobile, and one real business they're interested in.

**The stack** (released live, not emailed — attendance is the price):

| Element | Obstacle killed | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Off-Market Deal Flow Kit | #2 | $495 |
| Bankability Scorecard | #6 | $795 |
| Vendor Finance Term Sheet | #5, #9 | $1,200 |
| Board Credibility Packet | #3 | $950 |
| Their own deal structured live on screen | #10 | $1,500 |
| Lifetime access to Foundations | — | $2,000 |
| **Total** | | **$6,940** |

**Guarantee:** stay to the end and if you leave without a deal structure you can
use this month, tell us and we send $100 for your time.
*Qualifying conditions: live attendance, stayed past the close, one claim per
person, claimed within 48 hours by reply. Budget it as a real line item.*

**Scarcity:** live room capacity, stated honestly. **Urgency:** the next session
date and a countdown to it.

**Immediate-value move:** the Bankability Scorecard is delivered on
registration, not on the call. Time-to-first-value drops from seven days to
ninety seconds, and the scorecard is what makes them show up — they'll have run
a real business through it before the training starts.

## Rung 2 — $97: *The First Deal Starter Kit* (optional, ad-funding)

**Job:** self-liquidate ad spend and, more importantly, produce buyers. A
$97 buyer converts to the core program at several times the rate of a free
registrant, which is the real reason to run it.

**Contents:** Acquisition Thesis Builder, Normalised EBITDA Workbook, the LOI
and heads-of-terms templates, and a recorded teardown of three real structures
(one that funded, one that didn't, one that funded after restructuring).

**Price:** $97. **Guarantee:** unconditional 30-day refund, keep everything.

**Order bump ($47):** the Seller Call Recordings — six real first calls with
owners, annotated. Buy rate on bumps like this runs high because it answers the
one thing a template can't: what it actually sounds like.

**Only run this if** you have ad spend to recover. If you're selling to warm
audiences and referrals, skip the rung — it inserts a delay between interest
and enrolment for no gain.

## Rung 3 — $9,700: *The First Deal Accelerator* (core)

**Full name:** The 12-Month First Deal Accelerator — from no capital to an
accepted LOI, for professionals and owners buying their first or next business.

**Job:** the profit centre. Everything above exists to fill this.

### The stack

| # | Element | Obstacle | Delivery | Value |

| 3 | **Bankability Scorecard & DSCR Model** | 6 | Tool + training | $1,500 |
| 4 | **Document Library** — LOI, heads of terms, vendor note, SPA checklist | 9 | Counsel-reviewed templates | $5,000 |
| 5 | **Board Credibility Packet + advisor recruitment** | 3 | Templates + one live recruitment clinic | $2,500 |
| 6 | **Weekly Deal Clinic** — 50 weeks of live structuring | 10, 13 | Group call, deals reviewed on air | $12,000 |
| 7 | **Lender Desk introductions** — up to 3 per member | 7 | Warm intro, capped | $6,000 |
| 8 | **Due Diligence Pack** | 8 | Checklists + data room index | $2,000 |
| 9 | **Operator Hiring Kit** | 11 | Scorecards, comp, first-100-days | $2,500 |

| 11 | **The Deal Room** — cohort, pipeline board, peer JV | 14 | Community, 12 months | $2,000 |
| 12 | **Roll-Up & Exit Playbook** | 15 | Self-paced | $1,500 |
| | **Total value** | | | **$45,000** |

**Price:** $9,700 in full, or 3 × $3,700. Full-pay gets one extra private deal
review (a $2,000 element, and it moves 60–70% of buyers to full pay, which is
what the bonus is actually for).

### The guarantee — "Deal or we work free"

> Complete the milestones and if you haven't had an LOI accepted within twelve
> months, you keep everything and we keep working with you — free — until you
> do. If you'd rather walk, we refund the full fee.

Qualifying conditions, stated on the page and in the contract, not buried:

1. Deal Flow Engine built and live by day 30.
2. A minimum of 100 qualified owner approaches per quarter, logged in the deal room.
3. Every offer submitted scored on the Bankability Scorecard first.
4. Attendance at 75% of deal clinics, or the recordings logged as watched.

This is a conditional guarantee, and the conditions are the program. Anyone who
satisfies all four and still has no accepted LOI is someone we genuinely want
to keep working with, because the failure is ours. Anyone who doesn't satisfy
them has disqualified themselves in a way they can see and agree was fair.

**Risk on the other side:** an unconditional 30-day refund on top, no
conditions. The first 30 days are curriculum and templates — cheap to give
away, and it removes the "what if it's not what I think" objection that stops
people at the checkout rather than at the decision.

### Scarcity and urgency — must be true

- **Cohort of 25.** The constraint is deal clinic airtime: 25 members is what
  fits in 50 weeks of live structuring with every member's deal reviewed
  quarterly. That's a real number you can defend out loud.
- **Four cohorts a year.** Missing one means waiting a quarter.
- **Lender Desk capacity.** Introductions are relationship capital and run out;
  three per member, allocated in order of enrolment.
- **Price moves with proof.** Publish that the price rises with each cohort as
  case studies accumulate. Then actually raise it.

### Naming variants to test

- The 12-Month First Deal Accelerator *(clear, container-led)*
- Corporate to Owner in 12 Months *(avatar-led, strongest for the Corporate Prisoner)*
- The Zero-Capital Acquisition Program *(mechanism-led)*
- The 100-Approach Method *(process-led — owns a number nobody else claims)*

## Rung 4 — $25,000 + success fee: *The Deal Desk* (done-with-you)

**Full name:** The G&L Deal Desk — twelve months of advisory alongside a named
M&A advisor, for buyers acquiring $1M–$10M enterprise value.

**Job:** capture the buyers for whom time is worth more than money — the HNW
Capital Deployer and the Trapped Owner who already has cash flow and wants a
second and third business.

**What's different from Rung 3** — this is where the delivery vehicle changes
from taught to done:

- Deal flow **sourced and screened for you**, not built by you.
- A **named advisor** on every deal: structure, valuation, negotiation posture.
- **Represented** to lenders rather than introduced.
- Documents **drafted with counsel**, not templated.
- **Eight seats per quarter.** The constraint is advisor hours and it is real.

**Price:** $25,000 for twelve months, plus a success fee on completion.
⚠️ The success fee is the element that needs legal sign-off before it goes on
any page — transaction-contingent fees can bring licensing obligations
depending on jurisdiction and deal type. Have counsel confirm the structure, or
price it as a fixed annual retainer with no completion component.

**Guarantee — anti-guarantee, deliberately:** applications only, all fees
non-refundable, and say so plainly. At this level a refund promise *lowers*
perceived value: it signals that we expect people who need protecting. Replace
risk reversal with selection — an application, a screening call, and a stated
right to decline.

**Ascension beyond this:** for members with a platform acquisition completed,
a partnership on the roll-up — co-investment or carried interest rather than
fees. That's a capital structure, not an offer, and needs its own legal work.

## Ladder summary

| Rung | Name | Price | Guarantee | Constraint |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | No-Money-Down Acquisition Blueprint | Free | $100 for your time | Live room |
| 2 | First Deal Starter Kit | $97 | 30-day unconditional | None |
| 3 | First Deal Accelerator | $9,700 | Deal or we work free + 30-day | 25 per cohort |
| 4 | The Deal Desk | $25,000 + fee | Anti-guarantee, application only | 8 per quarter |

**Value-to-price ratio at the core rung: 4.6:1** ($45,000 stated against
$9,700). Hormozi's rule of thumb is a 10:1 perception. Close the gap by raising
perceived value honestly — publish the milestone timeline, name the lenders you
can name, and put real member deals on the page — rather than by inflating the
stack values, which sophisticated buyers in this market will price-check
against what a lawyer or a broker actually charges.
