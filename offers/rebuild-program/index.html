The Rebuild approach

  • A 12-week plan with every session booked in up front
  • Coached strength sessions in a dedicated rehab gym
  • Strength and function tested at weeks 0, 6 and 12
  • A plan to keep you strong after you graduate
How it works

Three phases. One measured outcome plan.

WEEKS 1–4

Settle & restore

Calm things down with hands-on treatment, then rebuild movement you can trust. You start within 72 hours of your assessment.

WEEKS 5–8

Load & strengthen

Progressive, coached strength work twice a week. Your week-6 re-test shows where you've gained and what to target next.

WEEKS 9–12

Return & perform

Get back to the specific things you've been avoiding, whether that's running, lifting, sport or work. Then graduate with a 90-day plan.

Is this for you?

Built for people who are ready to commit

This is a 12-week program with real time commitments, so it isn't for everyone. We'd rather tell you that up front.

A good fit if you…

  • Have had a back, neck, hip, knee or shoulder problem for more than 6 weeks
  • Are recovering from surgery and have been cleared to start rehab
  • Can attend about 3 sessions a week for 12 weeks
  • Want a measurable result, not just short-term relief

If you have a WorkCover, TAC, NDIS or DVA claim, apply anyway. We'll route you to the right funded pathway instead.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need a GP referral?

No. You can apply directly. If you're on a GP Chronic Disease Management plan, let us know on your application.

Can I claim this on private health insurance?

Eligible individual physiotherapy items may be claimable through HICAPS, depending on your fund and level of extras cover. We'll itemise what we can.

What happens after I apply?

A member of our team will call you, usually within one business day, for a short chat about your situation. If it sounds like a fit, we'll book your Rebuild Assessment at the clinic that suits you.

Why is there a limit on places?

Each client has a dedicated physio and coached gym sessions, so we can only take on a limited number of new clients each month without compromising care.

I've just had surgery. Is it too early?

We'll work with your surgeon's protocol. Mention your surgery and date on the application and we'll advise on the right start point.
