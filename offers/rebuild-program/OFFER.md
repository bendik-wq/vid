## 2. The offer architecture

### The "Grand Slam" core (Hormozi)

**Dream outcome:** "Get back to lifting, running, playing with your kids, and working without your [back / knee / shoulder] running your life."

The Hormozi value equation:

| Lever | How the offer moves it |
|---|---|
| Dream outcome ↑ | Sell "back to full strength and the life you've put on hold", not "physio sessions" |
| Perceived likelihood ↑ | Measured strength testing at weeks 0, 6 and 12 (dynamometer and functional scores), so progress is shown with numbers, not described in words |
| Time delay ↓ | Week 1 starts within 72 hours of the assessment. A 12-week fixed endpoint replaces an open-ended stream of "come back next week" |
| Effort & sacrifice ↓ | Every session is booked up front, the program lives in an app, there's a direct message line to the physio, and there are two locations with evening hours |

Kept within AHPRA rules (see section 6):

Do **not** promise outcomes ("pain-free in 12 weeks" or "or your money back"). See section 6.

## 3. The funnel (Jeremy Haynes-style application funnel)

```
Paid ads (Meta / YouTube / Google)  +  Organic short-form (Reels / TikTok / Shorts)
  → Landing page (VSL + value stack + "Programs from $2,997" pre-qualifier)
    → Application form (qualifies & disqualifies)
      → Booking (calendar) — only for qualified applicants
        → Pre-call nurture (SMS + email + "watch before your call" videos)
          → 15-min phone triage by setter / clinic coordinator
            → In-clinic Rebuild Assessment ($250, credited to the program)
              → Physio presents the plan → close (Rebuild / VIP / downsell)
```

Haynes's principles applied here:

1. **Show the price range on the page.** "Programs from $2,997" removes tyre-kickers before they reach a call. This trades lead volume for lead quality.
2. **The application is a filter, not a contact form.** Ask about location, problem, duration, what they've tried, their goal, start date, and readiness to invest. Unqualified leads go to standard booking, not the closer's calendar.
3. **Pre-call content.** Send 3–5 short videos between booking and assessment: the physio's philosophy, a walkthrough of a patient's measured strength data (de-identified, no testimonial claims), a gym tour, and "what happens at your assessment". This lifts the show rate and makes the close easier.
4. **Premium creative.** Shoot real footage at both clinics, with the physios explaining the "why". Avoid stock imagery.
5. **Speed to lead.** Call or text within 5 minutes of the application.

### Ad angles to test

1. **"Still doing the exercises from a sheet of paper?"** Contrast passive physio with a coached, measured strength program.
2. **"Your knee doesn't need another massage. It needs to get strong."** For the post-surgery and ACL crowd.
3. **"The 12-week program for [Melton / Williamstown] adults who are done 'managing' their back pain."** Location callout.
4. **"We measure your strength on day 1. Then we prove it changed."** The data-driven, likelihood-of-success angle.
5. **"Tradies: back on the tools, stronger."** Note that injured-at-work tradies go to WorkCover. This angle is for non-work injuries and chronic issues.

Use no before/after claims or patient testimonials in ads (AHPRA).

### Qualification criteria (setter checklist)

- Lives or works within ~30 min of Melton or Williamstown
- Musculoskeletal problem lasting more than 6 weeks, OR post-surgery (cleared by surgeon)
- Not a current WorkCover / TAC / NDIS / DVA claim (route those to the standard pathway)
- Wants to start within 30 days
- Has acknowledged the "from $2,997" investment

### Organic content engine (Daniel Iles / Viral Coach-style)

Iles's model is that going viral once is luck, but doing it every month is a system. That system runs on consistent, high-volume short-form video with conversion-focused ideas, scripted hooks, a fixed production cadence, and cross-platform repurposing. Here it serves as the low-cost top of the funnel, feeding the same application page as the paid ads.

**Production cadence**
- One 3-hour filming block per clinic per week, producing 15–20 vertical videos (Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts). The physios are on camera and an editor or VA cuts and schedules.
- Post 1–2 videos a day per clinic account, and cross-post every video to all three platforms.
- Each week, review the top performers by views, saves and profile clicks. Re-film winning ideas with new hooks, and hand the best 2–3 to paid as ad creative (a Haynes-style whitelisting approach).

**Content pillars** (each one ends with a soft call to action: "Link in bio to apply for the Rebuild Program")

| Pillar | Example |
|---|---|
| Myth-busting | "Rest isn't fixing your back. Here's what the research says" |
| Do this, not that | "3 knee exercises you're doing wrong after an ACL" |
| Measured proof (de-identified) | Real dynamometer readings from weeks 0 → 6 → 12, with no name or face and no outcome claims |
| Behind the scenes | Tour of the 300m² Melton gym, a Paramount Pilates session |
| Local | "Physio in Melton answers your questions", "Williamstown runners: watch this" |
| Physio point of view | "What I tell every tradie with a bad back" |

**Hook formulas to script against**
- Call-out: "If you're over 40 and your knee clicks on stairs…"
- Contrarian: "Stop stretching your tight hamstrings."
- Curiosity: "The test I do on every new back-pain patient."
- Mistake: "The #1 mistake people make after shoulder surgery."

**Tracking:** the link-in-bio URL carries `?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic` (and the equivalent for TikTok and YouTube). Organic and paid applications land in the CRM tagged separately, so cost per acquisition can be compared directly.

**AHPRA still applies to organic content:** no patient testimonials, no "pain-free in X weeks" claims, and general education only (not individual advice). Patient footage needs written consent and must not imply outcomes.

**Remotion opportunity:** this repo is set up for Remotion. Templated video formats (a branded hook intro, captions, a data-reveal animation for the strength numbers, an end card with the call to action) can be built once as compositions and re-rendered with new footage and text each week. That cuts editing time on high-volume output.

## 5. How it plugs into the lead-gen business

- The landing page posts each application (form-encoded) to a webhook. Point it at the CRM (GoHighLevel, HubSpot, a Zapier catch hook, etc.). All go-live settings are in `config.js`: webhook, per-clinic booking calendars, sales video, pre-call videos, Meta Pixel and GA4.
- Flow: 4-step application → `thank-you.html`. **Qualified** leads see their clinic's booking calendar and the pre-call videos. **Nurture** leads get a "we'll be in touch" message, and the setter offers the Foundations downsell or standard physio. **Funded** (WorkCover/TAC/NDIS/DVA) leads are told they'll be booked through the funded pathway. A `Lead` event fires to Meta (and `generate_lead` to GA4) on submit.
- Each lead includes `clinic` (Melton / Williamstown / Either) for routing to the right front desk, plus UTM parameters for ad attribution.
- To reuse the page for other clinics, swap the clinic block, address and prices. The offer structure carries over to any physio, chiro or allied-health clinic with a gym.

## 6. Compliance — read before launch

Physiotherapists are regulated by AHPRA. Section 133 of the National Law and AHPRA's advertising guidelines prohibit:

- **Testimonials** about clinical aspects of care. No patient reviews or "success stories" about outcomes on the page or in ads.
- **Creating unreasonable expectations of benefit.** No "pain-free guaranteed" claims and no outcome money-back guarantees.
- **Misleading claims or offers** (including gifts and discounts) that don't state their terms and conditions clearly.
- Encouraging **unnecessary use** of services.

That's why this offer guarantees *process* (assessment refund, session guarantee), not *outcomes*, and why the page uses de-identified measurement and method instead of testimonials. Have the clinic owners sign off on the final copy, and get a compliance review if possible.
