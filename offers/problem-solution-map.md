# Problem → solution → deliverable

Hormozi's step: list every obstacle between the prospect and the dream outcome,
turn each into a solution, then decide how to deliver it. Then trim anything
that is high cost to us and low value to them.

The right-hand column is where the bonus stack comes from. Nothing in any
stack is invented for the stack — it's an obstacle removal with a name.

| # | Obstacle (in their words) | Solution | Deliverable | Cost to us | Value to them |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "I don't know what kind of business to buy" | Give them a thesis instead of a menu | **Acquisition Thesis Builder** — sector screen showing which industries lenders fund, which they won't, and why | Low | High |
| 2 | "Every deal I see is overpriced and everyone's bidding" | Take them off-market | **Deal Flow Engine** — list-building criteria, owner outreach sequence, call opener, twelve qualifying questions | Med | High |
| 3 | "Sellers don't take me seriously" | Manufacture institutional credibility before the first call | **Board Credibility Packet** — advisor engagement letters, recruitment scripts, one-page buyer profile | Low | High |
| 4 | "I don't know what it's worth" | Replace opinion with a model | **Normalised EBITDA Workbook** — add-backs, owner comp normalisation, working capital peg | Low | High |
| 5 | "I have no money" | Show the capital stack where none of it is theirs | **Capital Stack Playbook** — vendor finance, senior debt, mezzanine, earn-outs, deferred consideration | Low | Very high |
| 6 | "The bank will say no" | Score bankability before the offer, not after | **Bankability Scorecard** — nine-minute DSCR and covenant pre-check | Low | Very high |
| 7 | "I don't know any lenders" | Hand them the relationship | **Lender Desk** — warm introductions to funders who have seen our structures before | High | Very high |

## Trim decisions

- **Keep and lead with**: 5, 6, 7, 10, 12. These are the high-value items
  competitors can't copy, because they require relationships and a licensed
  advisory arm rather than a camera.
- **Keep, low cost**: 1, 3, 4, 8, 9, 13, 15. Build once, deliver forever —
  these carry the stack's stated value at near-zero marginal cost.
- **Ration**: 2, 11, 14. Real ongoing cost. Deliver group-wise at the core
  tier, done-for-you only at the deal desk tier.
- **Do not offer at any tier**: sourcing deals *on behalf of* a member for a
  fee without the licensing question settled (see `README.md`).
